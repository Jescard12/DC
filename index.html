About D&C

One team. Two locations.

D&C Clinics isn't two separate practices that happen to share a name — it's one dental team, working across two locations in Byblos and Bikfaya. Dr. Dany Merhej and Dr. Celine Gemayel both treat patients at each location, alongside our orthodontist, rotating between the two so the same standard of care is on-site every day.

Because the team rotates, booking is simple: pick whichever location suits you, tell us which doctor you'd like to see, and we'll confirm your appointment for the day they're scheduled there.
