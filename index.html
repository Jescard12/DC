About D&C

One rotating team, two clinics.

Same last initials, same values, same commitment to painless, unhurried dentistry — but two separate clinics, two separate teams on the ground, and two separate schedules to fit around your week.

Because both doctors treat patients at each location on a rotating basis, the doctor on-site can change from day to day. Choose whichever clinic is closer to you below, send your preferred day over WhatsApp, and we'll confirm exactly which doctor will be seeing you.
